How it works

Moving to Germany means rebuilding your entire everyday infrastructure — supermarket, doctor, dentist, hairdresser, bank — in a place you don't know yet. Doing that alone means thirty browser tabs; letting an agent do it alone means decisions about your life made without you.

This page is built for both of you: your AI agent operates it through WebMCP tools (search 80+ categories in parallel, fill the plan, read your notes back), while you see everything it does live on the page — and curate, veto and annotate with your own hands. The plan belongs to both of you.

Data comes from the "in meiner Nähe" network: 80+ agent-readable German business directories built from OpenStreetMap and Overture Maps, distance-sorted per postal code. Machine-readable endpoints: /llms.txt, /openapi.json, /index.md, /.well-known/mcp.json.
